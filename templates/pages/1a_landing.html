What this data can and cannot tell you

  • {{ fmt_int(caveats.rows_no_coverage) }} of {{ fmt_int(caveats.rows_coverage_total) }} vaccination records carry no coverage figure. They are stored as blanks, not nulls, so a naive average counts them as zero. This site excludes them instead.
  • {{ fmt_int(caveats.rows_above_100) }} records report coverage above 100%. Kept, not capped: doses given outside the target cohort are real.
  • {{ fmt_int(caveats.rows_suspicious_zero) }} of {{ fmt_int(caveats.rows_infection_total) }} case records are zero in every year for that country and disease. A genuine zero and an unreported year look identical here. Those records are flagged rather than counted as success, and the flag is an inference, not a fact.
  • {{ fmt_int(caveats.rows_unmatched_territory) }} vaccination records come from territories with no matching country entry. Shown as unmatched, never dropped.
  • {{ fmt_int(caveats.countries_no_economy) }} countries have no economic classification. They appear as "Not classified" in every economic breakdown rather than disappearing from it.
