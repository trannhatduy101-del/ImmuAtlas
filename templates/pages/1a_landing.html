Before you dive in

If coverage is this high, why do the cases keep coming?

{{ fmt_pct(facts.coverage_last) }} average reported coverage across {{ fmt_int(facts.n_countries) }} countries — and still, {{ fmt_big(facts.total_cases) }} cases on record.

Reveal the answer Hide the answer

Because an average hides where it fails. A country can sit near the top and still miss whole communities; a global mean can climb while outbreaks flare in the gaps. High coverage and persistent cases are not a contradiction — they are the same data read at two scales.

That gap between effort and outcome is what every page here is built to show — with the denominator behind each number always named.
