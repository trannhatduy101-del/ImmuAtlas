How this comparison works

Global rate
Total reported cases ÷ total population × 100,000, summed across the whole dataset for that disease and year. Population-weighted, so it is not thrown off by a small country with an extreme rate.
Country rate
Each country’s own cases ÷ its own population × 100,000, compared against that single global figure.
Who is shown
Only countries strictly above the global rate, ordered from highest to lowest. A country at or below the line is left out by design.
SQL and Python
SQL does the aggregation, the rate calculation and the comparison in a subquery; Python validates the two selections and formats the output.
