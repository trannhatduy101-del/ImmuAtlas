Our Mission

How to Use ImmuAtlas

Explore immunisation data in three simple steps.

01

Choose a View

Select a data page based on the question you want to explore.

02

Apply Filters

Select relevant years, countries, infection types or economic groups.

03

Explore & Compare

Review the results, rates and summaries to identify useful patterns.

Tip: Start with the data pages to explore vaccination and infection information at different levels of detail.
